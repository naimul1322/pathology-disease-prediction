Why Decision Tree is the Best Model

  • Superior Predictive Performance: Outperformed all other 4 machine learning algorithms, achieving the highest Exact Match Accuracy (97.11%) and Weighted F1-Score (98.57%).
  • Optimal Regularization: Tuned with hyperparameters max_depth = 3 and min_samples_leaf = 15 to eliminate overfitting while maintaining sharp generalization on unseen patient data.
  • Simultaneous Multi-Output Classification: Wrapped inside MultiOutputClassifier to independently and concurrently detect all 8 pathology conditions.
